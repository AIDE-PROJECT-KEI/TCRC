# TCRC— Theoretical Coherence & Recursive Convergence

A mathematical research framework for structural coherence in adaptive intelligence systems,
and the mathematical basis of **SRE (Structure Reasoning Engine)** in AIDE PROJECT.

**Paper:** Research Paper No.19 — *Theoretical Coherence & Recursive Convergence — Public Research Edition*
**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23497266
**Author:** Keiichi Shiraishi (AIDE PROJECT) · ORCID 0009-0003-6511-8210

## Core ideas
- **Coherence and convergence are distinct.** A system can converge to an incoherent state,
  or stay coherent without converging.
- **Structural deviation is measurable** as distance from a set of coherent states.
- **Unresolved is not false.** Insufficient evidence is kept as an explicit unresolved status
  instead of being forced into a conclusion.

## Scope
The public edition organizes established results (fixed-point theory, invariant sets,
Lyapunov stability, three-valued information ordering) into one vocabulary and states its open problems.
It does not claim a general solution to coherence preservation.
